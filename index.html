How It Works

This game uses Succinct's SP1 zkVM to verify you've solved the maze without revealing your solution path.

The proof confirms you followed valid paths from start to exit, but keeps your exact route private.

×

How to Play zkMaze

Navigate through the maze from the start (green) to the exit (cyan) using the arrow keys or on-screen controls.

Once you reach the exit, generate a zero-knowledge proof that verifies your completion without revealing your actual path.
